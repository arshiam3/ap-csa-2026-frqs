# Question #3: Data Analysis with Arrays or ArrayLists

## My Approach:
I used two loops to cycle through each ArrayList. I tracked the id numbers of students in both lists, and used variables to track the absences students had in history and math. I then used an if statement that had to fulfill two bounds, the ids had to match (indicating the same student), and the number of history absences had to be greater than that of math. After both were confirmed, a counter variable was incremented by 1.
## Course Connections:
Projects like the temperature tracking projects introduced ArrayLists, however I strengthened my understanding of them through the FRQ practice we had done in class.
## What Gave Me Trouble:
I struggled with writing the loops to go through each arraylist, and I was also stumped in what I needed to write in the if statement to track the students with more absences in history than math.
## What I Would Do Differently:
Trying the question again, I would try to use enhanced for loops, as they require less coding for later lines of code.
